# Creative Direction

## Activation
Activate when interpreting creative briefs, deciding aesthetic direction (e.g. brutalist tech, warm editorial, futuristic glassmorphism, sleek minimalist), and establishing visual pacing.

## Style Archetypes
1. **Sleek Minimalist (Modern SaaS)**:
   - Palette: Deep slate/obsidian (`#090b10`), electric cobalt (`#3b82f6`), crisp white typography.
   - Pacing: Measured, razor-sharp, zero clutter. Easing: `expo.out`.
2. **Spatial Glassmorphism (Product Innovation)**:
   - Palette: Frosted glass transmission, iridescent edge highlights, soft multi-point volumetric lighting.
   - Pacing: Fluid, dimensional, cinematic camera pan.
3. **High-Velocity Kinetic (Social / Launch)**:
   - Palette: High-contrast monochrome with vivid neon punch accent (e.g. lime `#10b981` or magenta `#ec4899`).
   - Pacing: Rapid 0.8s hook, punchy typography cuts, snappy overshoot.

## Critique Checklist
- Does the opening 1 second arrest visual attention?
- Is the product/brand benefit unmistakable within 3 seconds?
- Does the resolution leave a memorable, clear brand impression?
